    Amascut, the Devourer

    Introduction

    The aim of this guide is to provide a basic overview of how to get consistent kills at Amascut, the Devourer. Due to the boss' high defence levels, demanding DPS checks and mechanical complexity, contents of this guide assume you have at least made it to the end of Stage 1 of #upgrade-order for your chosen style. As Amascut is enrage-based with fixed enrages of 100, 500, 750, 1000, 2000 and 4000%, mechanics and strategies may differ based on enrage. For the purposes of this guide, 100% enrage will be utilized.

    Disclaimer

    This guide, and its contents thereof, assume you are getting started with Amascut, and does not necessarily cover meta strategies.

    General Information

    ⬥ Amascut, the Devourer is located in Menaphos. Fighting her requires completion of the Eclipse of the Heart quest.

    ⬥ She has variable LP based on chosen mode and enrage:

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Normal Mode: 4.0M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 100%: 5.5M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 500%: 7M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 750%: 8M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 1000%: 9M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 2000%: 12M

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• 4000%: 16M

    ⬥ Defensively, Amascut has a Defence level of 95, with 2765 Armour and an affinity of 55 Affinity against all styles.

    ⬥ Amascut is immune to stuns and poison .

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Her minions can be poisoned and stunned.

    ⬥ Amascut is mechanically complex, consisting of 5 phases in Normal and 7 phases in Enrage mode.

    Gear, Relics, Familiars

    The following presets are based on Stage 1 and provided by Amascut, the Devourer Discord:

    ⬥ Necromancy

    ⬥ Magic

    Fight Mechanics (Phases 1 & 3)

    ⬥ Amascut attacks with and , with a mechanic every 3 auto attacks

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Magic attacks deal damage to all players, and is telegraphed by Amascut raising her left hand and shooting out a green ball of energy

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Melee attacks deal damage to whoever has Amascut's aggression, and any player within 2-3 tiles near them; telegraphed by Amascut raising her right hand and slashing her target

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ Melee attacks will additionally cause arrows to fly at anyone within 10 tiles of her target once the melee hit lands

    ⬥ Every 4 auto attacks, Amascut will perform Rapid Swipes where she starts viciously swiping at her target, dealing 2 quick hits of or damage based on her last used style, followed by a double hit that inflicts both types of damage

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In higher enrages, this has massive KO potential

    Attack Rotation

    ⬥ This rotation assumes Amascut is not phased by reaching her hitcap

    ⬥ 3 auto attacks → Tri-colour Attack → Rapid swipes + 2 auto attacks → Summon Scarabs → 1 auto attack + Rapid swipes + 1 auto attack → Soul Crush → 2 auto attacks + Rapid swipes → Triple Strike → 3 auto attacks → Tri-colour Attack → Rapid swipes + 2 auto attacks → Hard phase (Amascut enters P2 or P4 depending on which phase the players were on)

    ⬥ If the players reach phase hitcap for P1 and/or P3, Amascut will reset her mechanics rotation (starting over with Tri-colour attack being her first mechanic), but will not reset her auto attack rotation (if she entered P2 after landing Rapid swipes, her P3 rotation will start with 3 regular auto attacks)

    Corruption

    ⬥ Persistent mechanic that increases all incoming damage by 10% per stack

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Capped at 15 stacks for a total of 150% damage increase

    ​

    Sources:

    ⬥ Amascut's auto-attacks against her main target each inflict a stack

    ⬥ Getting hit by Devourer's Shockwaves or Soulfire Waves inflict a stack

    Tri-colour Attack

    ⬥ Amascut will flash 3 orbs in order, one for each style, and attack the Base Tank in the order she flashed the orbs

    ⬥ The attacks deal heavy damage if not prayed against, and will stun the player taking them at 500% enrage or higher

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Amascut telegraphs which styles she will attack with when she performs the mechanic:

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Pathetic: → →

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Weak: → →

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Grovel: → →

    ⬥ protects against being stunned at 500% enrage or higher

    ⬥ Only affects the Base Tank, other roles are safe

    Summon Scarabs

    ⬥ Amascut summons 2-5 scarabs per player based on enrage, charging up over time

    ⬥ The scarabs are Immune to damage until they are stunned

    ⬥ Once fully charged, they explode, dealing soft typeless damage to all players in the arena

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Their damage can be mitigated entirely by casting

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If unavailable, + can be used as a last resort

    ⬥ Stepping on them as they spawn will prevent them from spawning entirely, doing so will reset the cooldown of

    ⬥ Stunning them will lift their damage immunity and kill them due to their low health (2000 LP)

    Soul Crush (a.k.a. Bend the Knee)

    ⬥ Amascut deals massive soft typeless damage to her main target, split between all players in a 3x3 radius of her target

    ⬥ Has 50% piercing against defensive abilities

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Base Tank can at the end of Tri-colour Attack at earliest to negate this mechanic, assuming they do not proc it earlier

    ⬥ DPS pile can Soul Crush and split it between themselves, using + to stay alive

    Triple Strike

    ⬥ Amascut alters the skybox colour and prepares to launch 3 devastating hits with the same style she calls out

    ⬥ Not praying against the hits correctly deals massive damage

    ⬥ Affects all players in the encounter

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Amascut telegraphs which style she will attack with when she performs the mechanic:

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• All strength withers ‎(skybox turns red): 3 hits

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• I will not suffer this ‎(skybox turns green): 3 hits

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Your soul is WEAK ‎(skybox turns blue): 3 hits

    ⬥ will fully protect you from all 3 hits, as long as you are praying against the style correctly

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If timed correctly, / can gain a massive amount of healing and adren respectively from the third hit

    Fight Mechanics (Phases 2 & 4)

    ⬥ Amascut stops attacking, becomes Immune to all damage, and charges a wipe mechanic

    ⬥ She will invoke the names of two of the Bau of Tumeken (Apmeken and Crondis in Phase 2, Scabaras and Het in Phase 4), causing their respective mechanics to become active

    ⬥ Failure to complete this phase in less than 200 seconds will result in a WIPE

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• This timer is reduced to 125 seconds at 1000% enrage, and further reduced to 100 seconds at 2000%

    Statues & Fragments

    ⬥ The statues of the Bau of Tumeken are corrupted, and must be cleansed and restored to progress the fight

    ⬥ Clicking on the statue while they are glowing green will deal hard typeless damage to all players in the encounter

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ The statues can be cleansed by clicking on them once they start glowing golden (indicating a Chain inside was brought to 0 HP), doing so three times will cleanse the statue, allowing it to be restored

    ⬥ The statues can be restored by bringing its respective Fragment of Tumeken to them, this will stagger Amascut, interrupting her channelling and removing her damage immunity, allowing the encounter to be progressed

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once both statues for that phase are restored, the phase ends when Amascut is damaged to her hitcap, or once she fully recovers

    Portals (Inside Rooms)

    ⬥ In order to cleanse the statues, players must enter through a portal on each side of the arena, guarded by a Gatekeeper gorilla akh

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Gatekeeper gorilla akh has crawling speed, can potentially stun players with attacks

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once inside, the players must work together and towards killing the Chainwarden mage akh and the chains preventing the players from claiming a Fragment of Tumeken

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎⬩ The chains, once they drop to 0 LP, must be cleansed to fully destroy them, and once all three are destroyed, the Fragment of Tumeken can be claimed to restore its respective statue

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Gatekeeper gorilla akh can be dispatched relatively easily with high burst damage

    ⬥ Inside (Chains) and Outside (Statues) players must work together to take out the Chainwarden, Chains player takes out two chains and carries the Fragment of Tumeken, while the Statues player takes out one and cleanses their side's statue

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In Phase 4, both players remain inside as the Base Tank can singlehandedly cleanse both statues due to their proximity

    ⬥ Chains player runs the Fragment of Tumeken across the arena, the correct statue to restore is on the opposite side of where they entered through the portal (e.g. East player must ferry the fragments to the Western statues)

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• While carrying the Fragment of Tumeken, , & are disabled, can still be utilised

    Apmeken's Burden

    ⬥ Shortly after Phase 2 starts, Amascut marks one random player who is outside with Apmeken's Burden, a hard hitting bleed that deals typeless damage

    ⬥ The bleed wears off after a while, length based on enrage

    ⬥ If Apmeken's Burden deals damage to a player for the 8th time (indicated by the number of stacks on the debuff), that player is instakilled

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Players can pass Apmeken's Burden around to reset its stacks, to pass the debuff the player must be standing in a 1 tile radius of the player who never got the burden during the phase when its damage ticks

    ⬥ Should the debuff be applied, it can be safely ignored in enrages less than 2000% as it will never instakill

    ⬥ Apmeken's Burden can be skipped entirely, if Amascut cannot find a target when she tries to mark a player (e.g. all players are inside the portal) the debuff will never be applied

    Modest Crocodile Demons

    ⬥ At the start of Phase 2, and again once the statues are restored, two Modest crocodile demons will spawn, one for each side of the arena

    ⬥ Modest crocodile demons primarily attack with , sometimes attacking with if their target is too close

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Once the adrenaline bar above their head is charged, they fire a attack that cannot miss, draining the player's adrenaline by 20% and halving their adrenaline gain for a short time

    ⬥ These creatures have the Modesty and Provoke Immune buffs, reflecting damage from enhanced and ultimate abilities, as well as special attacks while preventing from changing their targets

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Pray against their attacks and use basic abilities and conjures to kill them

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Necromancers are strongly recommended to as it will speed up the kills

    ⬥ They will attack the first target they see until they lose them; as they are Provoke Immune, Statues roles should have their aggro once the second set spawns, Base Tank can tank/dispatch the first set

    Healing in Strength

    ⬥ Amascut will heal for a portion of all damage dealt by her minions (except for the Chainwarden mage akh) during Phase 4

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ This mechanic can be safely ignored as the healing is usually negligible

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If going for You Shall Not Heal Combat Achievement, / / is strongly recommended to stop Feline guard akhs and Gorilla gatekeeper akhs from dealing damage with their attacks

    Explosive Scarab Akhs

    ⬥ During Phase 4, Amascut will summon Explosive scarab akhs with 5000 LP and no damage immunity

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• In addition to the scarabs, Gorilla gatekeeper akhs, Chainwarden mage akhs and Chains all gain damage immunity through the Carapace buff

    ⬥ On death, the scarabs will start filling a bar above their head, causing them to explode once filled dealing soft typeless damage to all players in the arena

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Any remaining scarabs at the end of Phase 4 will simultaneously die and start charging

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Scarabs can be grabbed and tossed at targets once they have been killed and start charging their explosion, doing so will lift the Carapace buff from enemies (green glow around them)

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• To throw a scarab, click on a dead scarab to grab, then click on a target to throw it

    ⬥ At the start of Phase 5, use to null all of their damage as they explode

    Floor Mechanics (Devourer's Shockwaves, Soulfire Waves, Devourer's Hieroglyphs)

    ⬥ Amascut will blast the ground with high damage Shockwaves, Hieroglyphs or Soulfire Waves, similar to Nakatra in Sanctum of Rebirth

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Shockwaves have a fixed pattern and deal typeless damage, disabling defensive abilities and putting them on cooldown for 6s

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Soulfire Waves sweep the arena in a set pattern dealing high damage and lowering the duration of

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Hieroglyphs split the arena into 9 5x5 tiles, dealing heavy typeless damage and disabling defensive abilities as well as putting them on cooldown for 6s

    ⬥ Shockwaves appear in Phases 2 (West side), Phase 3, Phase 4 (East side) and Phase 7

    ⬥ Soulfire Waves appear in Phase 2 and Phase 4

    ⬥ Hieroglyphs appear in Phase 7 only

    Solution:

    ⬥ All floor mechanics are telegraphed, move out of the way if possible

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• For Soulfire Waves, praying will mitigate some damage, and both block one hit, can block hits while praying but will suffer duration reduction

    ⬥ In Phase 7, dodging Shockwaves and Hieroglyphs will reset the cooldowns of all movement abilities

    Fight Mechanics (Phase 5)

    ⬥ Amascut will go Immune once again, and summons a pair of soul obelisks that slowly charge towards periodic bursts of damage

    ⬥ To progress the fight, the players must work together to charge the Fragments of Tumeken freed in Phases 2 and 4

    ⬥ There is no timer in this phase

    Altar Charging

    ⬥ Four fragments of Tumeken, two on each side of the arena, will appear directly across the statues

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Far fragments are Crondis and Apmeken, and can be charged by the Solo Charge role

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Near fragments are Scabaras and Het, and must be charged by the South Charge and Interceptors

    ⬥ To charge a fragment, the players must form a bridge to carry Tumeken's light shooting out of the statues

    ⬥ Once at least 1 fragment is charged, players can click on Tumeken to start reforming him

    Soul Obelisks

    Altar Strike

    ⬥ So long as any altars are charging, Amascut will say Your light will be snuffed out once and for all ‎and launch 1-4 arrows at the altars based on how many altars are charging, dealing heavy damage and reducing a fragment's charge by a significant amount

    The God of Destruction (a.k.a. Blinking strikes)

    ⬥ As soon as Tumeken starts reforming, Amascut becomes damageable and blinks to a random side of the arena (North, South, West or East), dealing damage and stunning any player in her way

    ⬥ During this stage, Amascut will charge four devastating attacks (once per jump) that will deplete Tumeken's reformation progress

    ⬥ If not intercepted properly or intercepted at all, it will set back Tumeken's reformation progress and deal heavy soft typeless damage to all players as well as stunning them

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If Tumeken's life bar depletes, the players WIPE

    Solution:

    ⬥ Tumeken will call a player to intercept the attack by saying <playername>, shield us from her shadow...​

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• The player who is called out will notice their skybox turn golden

    ⬥ The player called out by Tumeken must be the first player to be hit by Amascut's attack, intercepting it and preventing potentially lethal damage

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If they are not the first player to get hit, the attack will deal damage as if it wasn't intercepted at all

    ⬥ All players who are standing in a line between Amascut and Tumeken will take typeless damage and have the cooldowns of all mobility abilities reset, allowing easy repositioning

    ​

    Note: Tumeken will never call the same player to intercept twice in a row

    Fight Mechanics (Phase 6)

    ⬥ Amascut will summon an Engulfing shadow that consumes Tumeken, constantly bombarding the arena with projectiles that deal heavy typeless damage

    ⬥ To progress the fight, the players must follow the Path of Tumeken and take Tumeken's light

    The Path of Tumeken (a.k.a. Platforms)

    ⬥ The center hieroglyph either on west or east side of the arena will start glowing, indicating that a player can follow the Path of Tumeken

    ⬥ The player following the Path of Tumeken (climbing) must kill four mage/range akhs in 30 seconds

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• If the collapse timer runs out, the climber is instakilled , and Amascut will deal however much black health she has as typeless damage to players, split between all players in the arena

    ​

    Solution:

    ⬥ Akhs have only 15k LP, high burst damage will kill them in one or (at worst) two hits

    ⬥ While climbing, the climber will see a glowing hieroglyph under the platform they're standing on, if another player stands on the same glyph the climber calls out, the collapse timer will be paused until the next platform

    ⬥ Once the four akhs are dead, the climber can click on Tumeken's light to finish climbing and gain the Enlightened buff, for the remainder of the phase they are Amascut's main target

    Shadows of Amascut (a.k.a. Clones)

    ⬥ Once Tumeken's light is claimed by the climber, Amascut will summon a Shadow of Amascut for each player alive who isn't the climber

    ⬥ If outside melee distance, they will dash at their target dealing damage, if in melee distance they will swipe at their target dealing damage

    ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎• Necromancy : Aim to enter Phase 7 with at least 8 and 4

    ⬥ After 4 auto attacks in the first cycle and 3 auto attacks in subsequent cycles, Amascut will address one of the Bau of Tumeken by name and face Tumeken directly, charging an extremely powerful attack
