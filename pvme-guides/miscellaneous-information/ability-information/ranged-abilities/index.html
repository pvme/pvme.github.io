    Introduction

    Note: Use the Table of Contents present at the bottom of the channel, or in the pinned comments, to quickly navigate through this channel and find the information you are looking for.

    ​

    Some general notes:

    ⬥ The game runs on an internal tick system: 1t (tick) = 0.6s (seconds).

    ⬥ The minimum time between abilities is called the global cooldown: 1 gcd = 3t = 1.8s.

    ⬥ Debuff spells like Vuln or Enfeeble or Smoke Cloud do not give adrenaline.

    ⬥ Some useful sheets and resources are given below.

    ​

    Ability Damage Maths

    Ability Hit Timings
    To see detailed breakdowns on ability hit timings, click here. This refers to when hitsplats appear on your target.
    Auto-attack Adren Gain
    To see adrenaline gained with different auto attacks, click here.

    Note: If you have any difficulties in using the tools or understanding what the information means, you can always ask in #pvm-help.

    Ranged

    This section covers basic abilities, enhanced abilities, ultimate abilities, utility abilities and special attacks for Ranged.

    ​

    Basic Abilities

    Ranged
    ⬥ Cooldown: 0t (0s) ⬥ Affected by Fleeting Boots     • Applies the cooldown reduction effect of

    ​

    Piercing Shot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 5t (3s) ⬥ Fires 2 hits ⬥ Reduces cooldown of Snipe by 4t (2.4s) per hit, 8t total (4.8s) ⬥ Affected by Fleeting Boots     • Reduces cooldown of by additional 2t (1.2s) per hit, 4t (2.4s) extra, 12t (7.2s) total

    ​

    Binding Shot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 25t (15s)     • Charges: 2 ⬥ Stuns for 2t (1.2s) and binds for 16t (9.6s) ⬥ It is affected by :     • Increases Max by 40% per rank     • Increases Minimum by 8% per Rank     • No longer stuns or binds

    ​

    Galeshot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 34t (20.4s) ⬥ Applies Searing Winds to self for 10t (6s) ⬥ Searing Winds:     • Ranged attacks deal 20% damage with each hit

    ​

    Ricochet
    ⬥ Cooldown: 17t (10.2s) ⬥ It is an AoE:     • Hits within a 5 square radius from SW tile of initial target     • Hits 3 targets, inclusive of main     • Main target hit an additional 15-20% for each enemy that cannot be found     • Does not hit things on initial target's SW tile ⬥ It is affected by Caroming     • Ricochet hits 4% bonus damage per Caroming rank ⬥ Not affected by Chinchompas

    ​

    Greater Ricochet
    ⬥ Cooldown: 17t (10.2s) ⬥ It is an AoE:     • Hits within a 5 square radius from SW tile of initial target     • Hits 7 targets, inclusive of main     • Main target hit an additional 15-20% (4-6% after 2 hits) for each enemy that cannot be found     • Does not hit things on initial target's SW tile ⬥ It is affected by Caroming     • Ricochet hits 4% bonus damage per Caroming rank ⬥ Not affected by Chinchompas

    ​

    Enhanced Abilities

    Snap Shot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 0t (0s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 25% ⬥ Two separate, strong hits

    ​

    Snipe
    ⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 0% ⬥ It is a channeled ability ⬥ It is affected by Nightmare Gauntlets , details in #armour-and-weapons

    ​

    Bombardment
    ⬥ Cooldown: 9t (5.4ss) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 25% ⬥ It is an AoE:     • 5x5 with target's true centre (SW tile) as centre     • Up to 9 targets ⬥ Not affected by Chinchompas

    ​

    Rapid Fire
    ⬥ Cooldown: 34t (20.4s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 25% ⬥ 8 hits ⬥ Binds for 10t (6s) ⬥ Channelled ability ⬥ Each attack extends the duration of Searing Winds by 1t (0.6s)

    ​

    Shadow Tendrils
    ⬥ Cooldown: 75t (45s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 0% ⬥ Calculates damage on activation ⬥ Deals self-damage to the player ⬥ Extends the duration of Shadow Imbue by 3.6s ⬥ Guaranteed to crit

    ​

    Corruption Shot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 25t (15s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 20% ⬥ It is a DoT:     • Not affected by or     • Hits every 2t (1.2s), for a total of 5 times ⬥ It is an AoE:     • Spreads to targets within 2 tiles each hit, up to 5 additional targets

    ​

    Imbue: Shadows
    ⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 40% ⬥ Applies Shadow Imbued to self for 50t (30s) ⬥ Shadow Imbued:     • Ranged attacks against your target generate 5% adrenaline for each hit

    ​

    Eldritch Crossbow
    ⬥ Special Attack: Split Soul ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 25% (22.5% with )     • Lasts 25t (t0-t24)     • Anything would have healed whilst spec is active instead deals Blight damage equal to 4 * heal amount     • To get the Split Soul damage both the heal and the hit of the ability must occur within the duration of the special     • Special attack is lost if main hand weapon is switched while active     • Blight damage is affected a second time by Vulnerability , X Slayer sigils , the NopeNopeNope PoF perk, and X Slayer perks

    Ultimate Abilities

    ​

    Deadshot
    ⬥ Cooldown: 50t (30s) ⬥ Adrenaline Cost: 60% ⬥ Fires 4 hits ⬥ Affected by the Igneous Kal-Xil and Igneous Kal-Zuk     • Adrenaline cost becomes 60%     • Fires 8 hits

    ​

    Death's Swiftness
    ⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ It is an on cast buff ⬥ Increases damage by 50% from t0→t51 (52t/31.2s duration); buff starts t1 ⬥ It has a 7x7 buff radius ⬥ It does not incur the auto cooldown ⬥ Planted Feet increases duration to t0→t64 (65t/39s)

    ​

    Greater Death's Swiftness
    ⬥ Cooldown: 100t (60s) ⬥ Duration becomes t0→t64 (65t/39s duration); buff starts t1     • Using does not extend this further ⬥ retains the DoT effect of     • Using will cancel this

    Escape
